For each dated unit the model is fitted leave-one-out, giving a likelihood

$\sigma_L$, mode $\mathrm{MAP}_L$).  Multiplying that likelihood by

likelihood, $\sigma_L^{-2}/(\sigma_L^{-2}+\sigma_u^{-2})$.  Where the
scholarly date is tightly specified the share falls below 10\%, and the
reported error collapses toward zero while the error from the evidence
