── INTERPRETATION ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HB-VI propagates uncertainty in (α, β, σ) into date posteriors,
  producing wider but better-calibrated CIs than MLE-MVN.
  MLE-MVN CIs (< 5 yr) are prior-dominated; they do not reflect
  genuine feature-driven uncertainty.
  Holdout MAE: HB-VI vs. MLE-MVN shows dramatic improvement because
  MLE-MVN mis-assigns holdouts to wrong register groups.
  Sub-source dates: archaic poems (Song_Sea, Song_Deborah) are
  bounded below by the training range (~800 BCE); the model correctly
  acknowledges it cannot extrapolate to truly pre-monarchic dates.
